# Third-Party Assets and Licenses

Every third-party asset used in Cinderbird is listed here, added in the same commit as the asset.

| Asset | Type | Author / Source | URL | License | Credit required | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *(example)* Nature Kit | Models | Kenney | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | CC0 | No | Background rocks |
| Liberation Sans (via TextMesh Pro Essential Resources) | Font | Red Hat / Liberation Fonts project, bundled by Unity | https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts | SIL OFL 1.1 (text in `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts/LiberationSans - OFL.txt`) | No | Debug HUD text |

## Accepted licenses
- **CC0 / Public Domain** — preferred, no credit needed
- **CC-BY** — allowed; must appear in the in-game credits
- **Royalty-free commercial** (e.g. Sonniss GDC bundles) — allowed; keep the license text in `/Docs/licenses/`

## Not accepted
- Non-commercial licenses (CC-BY-NC)
- No-derivatives licenses (CC-BY-ND) for anything we modify
- Assets with unclear or missing license
